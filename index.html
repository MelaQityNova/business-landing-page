02 · Biomaterials

Melanin first. A broader platform ahead.

Our flagship programme focuses on BSF-derived melanin. Other biomass fractions are part of the longer-term platform vision, subject to technical and economic feasibility.

Flagship development focus

Melanin

A naturally occurring pigment with promising UV-visible absorption, antioxidant and coloration properties. Potential uses include specialty pigments, research materials, coatings and personal-care ingredients.

Future recovery pathway

Chitin / chitosan

Potential recovery of structural biopolymers to expand the value obtained from BSF biomass, subject to process development and testing.

Longer-term opportunity

Additional fractions

Exploring further recoverable fractions as part of a more comprehensive biomass valorisation model and broader circular biotechnology platform.

03 · Development status

Progress built on testable milestones

Proof-of-concept has been achieved through initial laboratory extraction of melanin-containing material. Independent validation is the next step, alongside analytical characterization, reproducibility studies and scale-up feasibility assessment.

Proof-of-concept achieved

Initial melanin extraction

Early laboratory work has recovered melanin-containing material. Further characterization is required to confirm identity, composition and relevant performance.

Under development

05 · Next milestones

From proof-of-concept to independently evaluated materials

With proof-of-concept achieved, the next programme focuses on independent validation and reducing technical and commercial uncertainty before pilot-scale production.

STAGE 01

Define specifications

Identify minimum application-relevant product requirements and analytical acceptance criteria.

STAGE 02

Validate material

Advance extraction trials, independent characterization and impurity analysis.

STAGE 03

Test reproducibility

Compare repeated runs, assess yields and establish preliminary mass balance and cost drivers.

STAGE 04

Plan scale-up

Use technical evidence and customer feedback to define a suitable pilot pathway.
